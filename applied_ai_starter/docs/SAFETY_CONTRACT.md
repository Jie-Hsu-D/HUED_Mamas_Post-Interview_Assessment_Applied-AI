# Explanation-Only Safety Contract

The module may describe:

- The score tier returned by the already-computed scorer.
- Which approved domains contributed most strongly.
- Which domains were unavailable.
- That the output is descriptive, non-diagnostic, and limited by available inputs.

The module must not:

- Diagnose a condition or imply that a condition is present or absent.
- Tell a person what they should do, seek, change, take, stop, schedule, or discuss.
- Recommend treatment, testing, supplements, behavior, or provider action.
- Predict fertility, pregnancy, conception, treatment success, or clinical outcomes.
- Claim that an input caused an outcome.
- Invent evidence, citations, prevalence, thresholds, or clinical significance.
- Introduce facts that were not present in the bounded input.

A disclaimer does not make otherwise prohibited content acceptable. Safety must be enforced through bounded inputs, approved templates, validation, and tests.
